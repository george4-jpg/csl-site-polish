Ziggy is the canonical brand character for the Executive Intelligence Operating System (Monarch217). The official reference is the golden retriever/labrador puppy in a blue cape with a gold "Z" medallion (ZIGGY_1_PAGER_B). The avatar at src/assets/ziggy-avatar.jpg is a headshot crop of this canonical character.

Rules:
- Do NOT redesign, reinterpret, restyle, or replace Ziggy with another dog or artistic style. This character is canonical.
- Keep all pages, illustrations, icons, animations, onboarding, and marketing visually consistent with this Ziggy.
- Ziggy is the relationship layer; the operating system is the product. Ziggy is how humans experience it.
- Positioning phrase: "Meet Ziggy. Your Executive Intelligence Operating System." Tagline: "One trusted companion. Many capabilities. Human in control."
- Emotional qualities: happy to see the user, loyal, intelligent, trustworthy, protective, curious, calm, optimistic, professional, never judgmental. Visitors should feel "I trust this / this system is on my side / I want to meet Ziggy."

Forbidden: never use the phrase "AI Assistant". Prefer "Executive Intelligence Operating System" or "Executive Intelligence Companion". Maintain Monarch217 premium dark blue-and-gold visual language.
